# Binding the CAPS Key to Escape for (neo)vim

After looking for different solutions - and somehow losing the one that could have worked without extra software - I "settled" on [keyd](https://github.com/rvaiya/keyd) because it is recommended in many places.

Just follow the installation instructions on the [COPR Page](https://copr.fedorainfracloud.org/coprs/alternateved/keyd/).
